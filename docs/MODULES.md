# Module Documentation

## Branding
Two separate identities are used everywhere and never replace each other:
- **A. Medical brand** – Dr. Faraj Lashika, Orthopedic & Sports Medicine (login screen, sidebar, header, dashboards, every PDF header and footer).
- **B. Club logo** – the selected club (sidebar club section, header, club dashboard, player profiles, the club band of every PDF).

## Multi-club architecture
- `clubs` holds each club (auto Club ID `CLB-0001`, name, short name, logo, country, city, sport, category, league, season, contacts, notes, status).
- `club_members` assigns staff accounts to clubs. Users see only their clubs; Super Administrators see all clubs.
- Every operational table has a mandatory `club_id`. Child records (follow-ups, treatments, sessions, findings, documents…) **inherit**
  `club_id` from their parent in the database, so it cannot be forged or changed through the API. Players, seasons and exposure
  records cannot be moved to another club after creation.
- Player IDs and jersey-number uniqueness are per club. The same person may be registered in two clubs only as two explicit registrations.
- Deactivated clubs keep all records (read-only for new registrations).

### Club switcher
Sidebar (desktop) and header (mobile) show the current club with its logo. Switching club resets every page and applies the
new club to every query. Detail pages of the previous club return to the matching list.

### Dashboards
- **All Clubs Overview** – totals across authorised clubs (clubs, players, active, injuries, currently injured, rehabilitation,
  available, examinations) plus a club comparison chart and table.
- **Club dashboard** – statistics for the selected club only.

## Dashboard
Live statistics computed from database records (registered, medically cleared, full training, injured, under rehabilitation,
modified training, awaiting assessment, clearance pending), 7 analytics charts, recent activity and upcoming follow-ups.
Date filters: last 7 / 30 days, current month, current season, custom range. Non-clinical roles see an availability-only dashboard.

## Players
Add/edit/search/filter, archive and restore (never deleted). Player ID `DCB-0001` is generated by the database.
Age and BMI are calculated automatically. Duplicate jersey numbers within the same active roster are blocked in the form **and** by a unique database index.
The profile has 10 tabs: Overview, Personal Information, Medical History, Examinations, Injuries, Treatments, Rehabilitation, Medical Clearance, Documents, Activity Timeline.

## Medical history
Versioned: every update creates a new version with a reason; previous versions remain viewable and are audited.

## Medical examinations
Pre-Season / Periodic / Post-Injury / Return-to-Play / Other. Vital signs with range validation, 6 systematic regions and 11 orthopedic regions
(normal/abnormal, findings, pain, ROM, strength, stability, special tests, notes), investigations with document upload, final assessment,
and a clearance decision that requires the physician's signature. Clearance is **never** automatic; a documented decision is copied to the permanent clearance history.

## Injury management
Fast, progressive form (≈1–2 minutes): player & event → anatomical location grid → type/diagnosis → optional clinical details → initial treatment → status & availability.
Custom injury types and mechanisms are allowed. Severity and grade are entered by the physician only. Possible recurrences are flagged for the physician to confirm.
Each injury has its own visual timeline, treatments, follow-ups, rehabilitation, RTP decisions and documents.

## Follow-ups
Pain score 0–10, swelling, tenderness, ROM, strength, function, findings, response, updated diagnosis, next date. Records are appended (never overwritten),
compared side-by-side and charted as a recovery timeline.

## Rehabilitation
Injury-linked programmes with 7 phases. Phase changes require named physician approval (enforced by a database trigger). Multiple sessions per programme with pain trend.

## Return to play
Criteria-based assessment (pain, ROM, strength, functional, balance, agility, jumping, landing, basketball-specific movement, tolerance) and a signed decision.
A decision clearing full training/competition sets the injury's return-to-play date (used for days-lost). Full history is permanent.

## Availability
Daily table with 5 categories. Each update is a new history row; the physician chooses whether it is shared with coaching staff. Printable + PDF daily report.

## Documents
PDF/JPG/PNG up to 10 MB, validated by file signature, stored in a private bucket, opened through 2-minute signed URLs, linked to player and optionally examination/injury. Archived, not deleted.

## Reports (PDF)
13 reports. Header: medical brand logo, physician name and specialty, then a separate navy band with the **club logo**, club name,
country and season, reference number and date of issue. Footer: *Dr. Faraj Lashika · Orthopedic & Sports Medicine · Official Team Medical Records*.
Logos are rasterised with their aspect ratio preserved. Reports include reference number, physician, signature block, page numbers and confidentiality footer; previewed before download.

## Export center
Scope: **current club** or **all my clubs**; every CSV starts with *Club ID* and *Club Name* columns. 11 CSV exports (UTF-8 with BOM, CRLF, formula-injection protection) with date, season, player, injury type, status and region filters, plus a physician-only full JSON backup. Exports are audit-logged.

## Analytics
Total/new/recurrent injuries, injuries per player, by region/position/mechanism, contact vs non-contact, match vs training, total and average days lost,
rehabilitation duration, RTP outcomes. Incidence per 1,000 player-hours is shown **only** when session exposure has been recorded.

## Security
RLS on every table · inactive-by-default accounts · users cannot change their own role · audit triggers on every clinical table (old/new values) ·
explicit audit events for logins, exports, backups and document views · sessions stored in sessionStorage with 30-minute idle sign-out ·
private storage only · no service-role key in the frontend · security headers via `vercel.json`.
